# Interpreting research papers without importing mythology

- Reviewed: 2026-08-05
- Evidence state: project methodology

A paper supports only the population, task, system, metric, comparator, budget, and assumptions it evaluates. Architecture diagrams are descriptions, not guarantees. Benchmark improvement is not automatically a safety result; repeatability is not convergence; a generated proof or program is not verified until the stated checker and assumptions are examined.

For Google DeepMind work, prefer the linked paper/project page and code/data artifact over summaries. Record publication/version, exact claim, experimental unit, evaluator, baselines, uncertainty, negative results, resource budget, and applicability to this repository. Keep quotations short and distinguish authors’ observations from our inference.

The [AlphaEvolve official description](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/) is interpreted here only as evidence for proposal/evaluator separation in executable domains. Reflective Continuum uses standard-library graph tests and is neither a model training system nor scientific replication.

A review must state counterevidence and a disconfirming condition. If no repository behavior depends on a paper, the reference remains context rather than normative policy.
